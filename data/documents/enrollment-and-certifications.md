# Enrollment and Certifications — LinguaBridge Academy

## How to Enroll
1. Take the free placement test (online or in-person), or skip it if you are a total beginner.
2. Choose your modality (in-person, live online, or self-paced) and preferred schedule.
3. Complete the registration form on the LinguaBridge website or at the front desk.
4. Pay the enrollment fee (see Pricing and Levels document) to confirm your seat.
5. Receive a confirmation email with your class schedule, platform login, and instructor information within 24 hours.

Enrollment can be completed at any time, but seats for a given cohort are only guaranteed until the registration window closes (the Thursday before the cohort start date).

## Certifications
LinguaBridge issues a Certificate of Completion for every level passed, automatically generated once a student:
- Attends at least 80% of live sessions (in-person or live online), or completes at least 90% of self-paced modules, and
- Scores 70% or higher on the final level assessment.

Certificates are digital (PDF, verifiable online with a unique code) and are emailed within 5 business days of finishing the level. Physical printed certificates can be requested for an additional COP 25,000.

LinguaBridge certificates are institution-issued and are not equivalent to official international exams (such as IELTS, TOEFL, DELF, or CELPE-Bras). Students preparing for an official international exam should ask about our dedicated exam-prep add-on modules.

## Cancellations and Transfers
- Students may transfer to a different modality or schedule once per level, free of charge, subject to availability.
- Refunds are available only within the first week of a cohort, minus a COP 50,000 administrative fee.
- After the first week, no refunds are issued, but students may pause and resume within the same level within 60 days.

## Support
For anything not covered by placement tests, schedules, pricing, or certifications, students should be connected with a human advisor via the academy's WhatsApp support line or front desk.
