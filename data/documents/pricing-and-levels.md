# Pricing and Levels — LinguaBridge Academy

## Language Levels
LinguaBridge follows the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR): A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2. Each level takes one course cycle to complete (8 weeks for in-person/live online, up to 12 weeks for self-paced).

All new students must take a free 20-minute placement test before enrolling, unless they are total beginners (A1), in which case the placement test is skipped automatically.

## Pricing (per level, in Colombian Pesos, COP)

### English
- In-Person: COP 480,000
- Live Online: COP 420,000
- Self-Paced Online: COP 280,000

### French
- In-Person: COP 520,000
- Live Online: COP 460,000
- Self-Paced Online: COP 300,000

### Portuguese
- In-Person: COP 500,000
- Live Online: COP 440,000
- Self-Paced Online: COP 290,000

## Discounts
- Paying for 3 levels upfront: 10% discount on the total.
- Paying for 6 levels upfront: 15% discount on the total.
- Referral discount: COP 30,000 off for both the referring student and the new student, applied to their next level.
- Students currently enrolled at a partner university receive a 12% discount with valid student ID.

## Payment Methods
Bank transfer, credit/debit card (via the online payment portal), or cash at the front desk (in-person academy location only). Payment plans of up to 3 installments are available for in-person and live online courses; self-paced courses must be paid in full.

## Materials
Digital course materials are included in the price. Printed textbooks (optional) cost an additional COP 45,000 per level and are only available for in-person students.
