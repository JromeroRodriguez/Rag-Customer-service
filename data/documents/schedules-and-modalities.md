# Schedules and Modalities — LinguaBridge Academy

LinguaBridge Academy offers English, French, and Portuguese courses in three modalities: in-person, live online, and self-paced online.

## In-Person Classes
Location: Calle 45 #22-18, Barranquilla, Atlántico.
- Monday to Friday, morning group: 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM
- Monday to Friday, evening group: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Saturday intensive group: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM
Each in-person level lasts 8 weeks, with classes held 3 days per week (Monday/Wednesday/Friday or Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday).

## Live Online Classes
Delivered via Zoom with a live instructor.
- Monday to Friday, two time slots available: 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM (Colombia time, GMT-5) and 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Colombia time, GMT-5)
- Saturday slot: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
Live online levels last 8 weeks, 3 sessions per week, same schedule logic as in-person groups.

## Self-Paced Online
Available 24/7 through the LinguaBridge learning platform. Students have 12 weeks to complete each level at their own pace, with optional weekly conversation clubs held every Wednesday at 6:00 PM (Colombia time).

## Holidays and Closures
The academy is closed on all official Colombian public holidays. Live classes falling on a holiday are rescheduled to the following Saturday at 9:00 AM. Self-paced platform access is never interrupted by holidays.

## Registration Windows
New cohorts start on the first Monday of every month. Registration closes the Thursday before the cohort start date.
